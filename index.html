01 — About Me

The rigor of clinical research and a writer’s clarity, applied to regulatory and medical documentation.

I’m an aspiring medical writer with over 2 years of clinical research experience encompassing clinical data management and regulatory documentation practices across site level, pharmaceutical, and academic exposures.
